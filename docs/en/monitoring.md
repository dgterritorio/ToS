# Service quality monitoring

DGT monitors the operation of its OGC APIs through monitoring mechanisms that make it possible to assess service availability, performance and compliance with the quality criteria defined for the services.

Monitoring results are used to identify outages, performance degradation and other situations that may affect service quality.

## Monitored indicators

The main indicators considered are:

- service availability and reliability;
- response time;
- service capacity.

## Reference period

Quality indicators are calculated based on the results obtained during the reference period defined for each service.

Results may vary over time depending on the behaviour observed during the monitoring period.
